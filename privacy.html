Privacy Policy

Follow Up is a bill split notification app. We collect user email addresses, phone numbers, bill details, participant names, and participant phone numbers to send bill reminder notifications.

We use SMS only to send bill reminders, payment status updates, and opt-out confirmations.

Users and participants may opt out of SMS reminders by replying STOP with the bill ID.

SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
